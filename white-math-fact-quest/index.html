Helpful reminders

  • Multiplication can be thought of as equal groups.
  • Facts with 0 always have a product of 0.
  • Facts with 1 keep the other factor the same.
  • You can use the commutative property: 4 × 7 and 7 × 4 have the same product.
  • Speed can grow with practice, but accuracy comes first.
