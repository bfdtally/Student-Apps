❓ About "Who Am I👤?"

Who Am I👤? is an educational game designed for 3rd, 4th, and 5th-grade Social Studies classrooms. By presenting history through interactive clues and customizable detective shop rewards, students stay engaged while learning key figures.

🔒 Safety & Privacy

This application operates completely locally in your browser. It does not harvest personal student data, require external database logins, or connect to third-party payment gateways.
