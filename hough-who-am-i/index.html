Grade Alignment & Accessibility

Designed specifically for upper elementary learners (Grades 3-5). High-contrast typography (Fredoka & Lexend fonts), soft warm pastel tones, generous tap targets, immediate feedback loops, and gamified badges make historical inquiry accessible and fun for all learners.
