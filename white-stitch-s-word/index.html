About the app

About & Help

What this app teaches

Stitch's Word World is a K–2 language arts learning app for practicing phonics, spelling, vocabulary, sentence skills, and early reading with Stitch as a learning buddy.

How to use it

Choose a learning level, pick a lesson, answer five short rounds, check a learning helper, and visit My Progress to see what to practice next.

Accessibility

Large controls, keyboard-friendly navigation, visible text alongside audio, replayable speech, responsive layouts, and no required audio.

Remember

Mistakes are part of learning. You can hear directions again, use a hint, replay an activity, or choose a different one.
